Model science: how language models are built, scaled, trained, and compressed.

**[Troiani](/llms/troiani/)** is the from-scratch build — a Mamba-3-dominant hybrid under 1B parameters.

- **Architecture** — transformers, attention, hybrids and SSMs
- **Scaling** — compute efficiency and power laws
- **Pre-training** — data, optimization, training tricks
- **Mid-training** — SFT, RLHF, tools and agents
- **Compression** — quantization, pruning, distillation
- **Retrieval & RAG** — embeddings and grounded generation
